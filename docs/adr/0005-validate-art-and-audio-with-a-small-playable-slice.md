# Validate Art and Audio With a Small Playable Slice

Before replacing the prototype visuals and generated sounds wholesale, Chicken Olympics will prove its intended higher-fidelity presentation through a tiny authored playable slice judged inside an active local match. The first slice is the normal claim loop: player chickens, normal chicks, a small number of hiding spots, low-contrast Farmyard Stadium framing, normal claim feedback, and claim SFX variants. The slice should use bespoke PNG spritesheets for the core match-readability objects and claim feedback, with player chickens starting from one shared recolorable base so color variants do not multiply animation work or create unfair silhouette differences. Sound should use a hybrid approach where a small authored SFX pack can play the important claim moments while the existing generated WebAudio remains useful fallback scaffolding. This deliberately avoids committing to a full art pipeline, asset-store kit, green-chick showcase, full arena replacement, busy decorative background, different player silhouettes, or all-authored sound migration until players can read player chickens, chicks, hiding spots, and claim ownership clearly at play speed.
